One corner of a cube: three lines, x, y and z

A point in 3D is three numbers: (x, y, z).
To get there from the corner: go x steps right, y steps up, and z steps into the screen.

The x line

From (0, 0, 0) to (200, 0, 0).

Only x changes. y and z stay 0.

The y line

From (0, 0, 0) to (0, 200, 0).

Only y changes. x and z stay 0.

The z line

From (0, 0, 0) to (0, 0, 200).

Only z changes. x and y stay 0.

But the screen is flat!

The screen only has two directions: right and down. Right and up are easy. For "into the screen" we use a trick: we draw it as a slanted line. Every step into the screen moves the pen a little to the right and a little up. This is how you draw a box on paper, too.

screenX = cornerX + x + z × slant
screenY = cornerY − y − z × slant

Why minus? On a screen, y counts down from the top. To go up, we take away.

Build your own point

Move the sliders. The dashed path walks to the point: first along x, then up y, then into z.
