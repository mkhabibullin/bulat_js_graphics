The math behind the car

Movement physics: acceleration changes speed; speed changes position. Rotation determines which way the car moves.

speed += acceleration × dt
x += cos(angle) × speed × dt
y += sin(angle) × speed × dt

One transformation matrix positions and rotates the *whole* car — wheels, lights, and windows together:

M = [ 1.00  0.00  0.00 ]
    [ 0.00  1.00  0.00 ]
    [ 0.00  0.00  1.00 ]

The matrix is M = Translation × Rotation. Everything in drawCar() is drawn in local coordinates around (0, 0). The car uses simple arcade movement, not a full tire or suspension physics model.
