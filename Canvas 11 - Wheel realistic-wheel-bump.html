The Rolling Wheel

One realistic-looking wheel. One simple road line. One jump.

Pure HTML + JavaScript · Offline
Rolling
01 · Artwork (once)

The tire, treads, metal rim, spokes and bolts are drawn on a hidden Canvas and saved as a reusable sprite.

02 · Matrices (every frame)

We combine T × R to move and spin the same sprite — no redrawing every spoke.

03 · Physics (every frame)

Horizontal speed advances the wheel; an impulse starts the jump; gravity brings it back to the road.

Learning demo: the jump is intentionally arcade-style, not a complete collision or suspension simulation. The road and bump remain a single line path.
