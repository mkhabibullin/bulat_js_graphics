The science inside the game

Horizontal motion: The ball keeps moving forward at a steady horizontal speed. Vertical motion: Gravity continuously pulls it downward.

x(t) = v × cos(angle) × t
height(t) = v × sin(angle) × t − ½ × g × t²

Real-world assumption: These equations assume a flat surface, uniform gravity, and no air resistance. They are an idealized but genuine physics model, not an invented animation path.
