Physics behind the game

vafter = −e × vbefore

The minus sign reverses direction. The coefficient of restitution e determines how much of the impact speed the ball keeps after bouncing.

hbounce = e² × hdrop

For example, e = 0.5 means the ball rebounds to only 25% of its previous height (in this simplified model). Gravity follows a = −g in our upward-positive height coordinates.

Try this challenge: Choose e = 0.5 and a drop height of 4 m. Predict the first rebound height before pressing Drop. Then change gravity. Does the rebound height change, or only how quickly the ball moves?

Sources: Coefficient of restitution (Wikipedia) · Bouncing-ball experiment (OpenStax).
