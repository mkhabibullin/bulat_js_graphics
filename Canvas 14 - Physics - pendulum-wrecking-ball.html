The real physics equation

Gravity makes the ball swing

angularAcceleration = -(gravity / length) * Math.sin(angle);

This is the nonlinear pendulum equation: α = −(g/L) sin(θ). The game updates angular velocity and angle many times per second. Its swinging motion is based on this equation, not an animation trick.

*Predicted bottom speed: v = √[2gL(1 − cos θ₀)] from conservation of energy. This ignores air resistance and any collision. Block toppling: an educational approximation using a collision impulse and rotation around a corner, not a full rigid-body physics engine.
